Reading the tags

  • No gluten ingredients as served, no changes needed
  • Any milk no gluten ingredients with any of the options shown
  • Swap normally comes with an option that has gluten, so ask for another
  • Ask no gluten ingredients only with the change shown
  • Wheat contains gluten whatever you order
  • Not suitable the restaurant says it isn't suitable for coeliacs because of how it's prepared
  • Ingredient an ingredient the guide lists on its own, not something to order. Rows marked Part of a dish are the same for parts such as burger toppings. Neither has a staff card
Staying up to date

The app checks for new menus each time you open it with signal. When a restaurant's guide changes, a note shows what's new, what's gone and anything that now contains gluten, and changed items are marked in the list. If a guide has passed its end date, you'll see a warning.

Show or hide

Everything shows until you choose. Tap the buttons under the search box to show only items with No gluten ingredients (as served, or with any milk except the ones named), items that Need a change (ask for a swap or a change), and items to Avoid (they contain gluten, or the restaurant says they aren't suitable because of how they're prepared). The Avoid list under the menu opens when you search or choose Avoid.

Favourites

Open any item and tap ☆ Save. Favourites stay on this phone. If a new guide puts a favourite on the Avoid list, or drops it, you'll see a warning at the top until you remove it.

Share my favourites makes a link holding them all: keep it as a backup, or open it on another phone to add them there. Anything that has since gone, or is now on the Avoid list, isn't added.

Show staff

Open any item and tap Show staff for a large-print card with the item, how to order it, and that you have coeliac disease. The screen stays lit while a card is open.

Share an item, or report a mistake

Open any item and tap Share this item for a link that opens straight to it. Tap Something wrong? (on any item or city place) for a ready message with the restaurant, item and guide page, to send to whoever looks after the app.

Berlin and Amsterdam

Tap Berlin or Amsterdam next to the title (or choose one from the title; the app also offers it when your phone is on that city's time) for places there that are all gluten free, by their own word or the coeliac society's list, or that say they make gluten free food in a separate kitchen. Each tag says who makes that claim. Each row says whether it's open now (local time). Open a place for its own words, its addresses with map links, opening hours, and anything to ask about, such as oats. Open near me shows what's still open today, nearest first; Still open today, Near me and ★ Saved narrow the list on their own. The German card or Dutch card chip opens the coeliac card, and About the tags and Before you go open under the introduction. Tap UK to go back to the menus.

Travelling

Menus in other languages show each item in English with the original name underneath, so you can point to it when ordering. A coeliac card you can show staff is available in: . Open it from a city's page, or from a restaurant whose menu is in that language.

I'm the app's owner

Uploading straight to the app needs a one-time GitHub key for this app's project. It's saved on this phone only.

  1. In Safari, open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new.
  2. Name it Treat Temple upload and pick an expiry.
  3. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick gf-drinks.
  4. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  5. Tap Generate token, copy it and paste it below.
