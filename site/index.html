Install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share. (On older iPhones, tap Share directly.)
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add. If it's off, you get a plain bookmark instead.

It opens full screen and works without signal using the last menus it downloaded.

Show or hide

Everything shows until you choose. Tap the buttons under the search box to show only items with No gluten ingredients (as served, or with any milk except the ones named), items that Need a change (ask for a swap or a change), and items to Avoid (they contain gluten, or the restaurant says they aren't suitable because of how they're prepared).

Reading the tags

  • No gluten ingredients as served, no changes needed
  • Any milk no gluten ingredients with any of the options shown
  • Swap normally comes with an option that has gluten, so ask for another
  • Ask no gluten ingredients only with the change shown
  • Wheat contains gluten whatever you order
  • Not suitable the restaurant says it isn't suitable for coeliacs because of how it's prepared
  • Ingredient an ingredient the guide lists on its own, not something to order. Rows marked Part of a dish are the same for parts such as burger toppings. Neither has a staff card

Favourites

Open any item and tap ☆ Save. Favourites stay on this phone. If a new guide puts a favourite on the Avoid list, or drops it, you'll see a warning at the top until you remove it.

Show staff

Open any item and tap Show staff for a large-print card with the item, how to order it, and that you have coeliac disease.

Staying up to date

The app checks for new menus each time you open it with signal. Tap ↻ to check now. When a restaurant's guide changes, a note shows what's new, what's gone and anything that now contains gluten, and changed items are marked in the list. If a guide has passed its end date, you'll see a warning.

Warnings

Each restaurant's own allergen warnings, word for word, plus what its guide doesn't tell you.

Berlin

Tap Berlin beside the search box (or choose it from the title) for places in Berlin that are all gluten free, by their own word or the Berlin coeliac support groups', or that say they make gluten free food in a separate kitchen. Each tag says who makes that claim. Each row says whether it's open now (Berlin time). Open a place for its own words, its addresses with map links, opening hours, and anything to ask about, such as oats. Still open today, Near me and ★ Saved narrow the list. The German card chip opens the coeliac card, and About the tags and Before you go open under the introduction. Tap UK to go back to the menus.

Travelling

Menus in other languages show each item in English with the original name underneath, so you can point to it when ordering. A coeliac card you can show staff is available in: . Open it from the Berlin page, or from a restaurant whose menu is in that language.

Add a menu

Send a restaurant's allergen menu (PDF or photos) to whoever shared this app, so they can add it. The app's owner can upload it straight to the app, where it's checked within a day and added once approved.

I'm the app's owner

Uploading straight to the app needs a one-time GitHub key for this app's project. It's saved on this phone only.

  1. In Safari, open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new.
  2. Name it Treat Temple upload and pick an expiry.
  3. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick gf-drinks.
  4. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  5. Tap Generate token, copy it and paste it below.
