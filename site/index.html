Add a menu

Know a place that should be here? Ask for it by name: its own allergen information is looked up and sent to the app's owner to approve. Or send its allergen menu (PDF or photos) to whoever shared this app. The owner can send either straight to the app, where it's checked within a day and added once approved.

Reading the tags

  • No gluten ingredients as served, no changes needed
  • Any milk no gluten ingredients with any of the options shown
  • Swap normally comes with an option that has gluten, so ask for another
  • Ask no gluten ingredients only with the change shown
  • Wheat contains gluten whatever you order
  • Not suitable the restaurant says it isn't suitable for coeliacs because of how it's prepared
  • Ingredient an ingredient the guide lists on its own, not something to order. Rows marked Part of a dish are the same for parts such as burger toppings. Neither has a staff card of its own; a part goes on an order instead (see Build an order)
Staying up to date

The app checks for new menus each time you open it with signal. When a restaurant's guide changes, a note shows what's new, what's gone and anything that now contains gluten, whether your favourites there are unchanged, and changed items are marked in the list. A restaurant new to the app gets a note too, and a New tag in the chooser until you open it. If a guide has passed its end date, you'll see a warning. The Home Screen app can also tell you about new menus with a notification (Tell me about new menus, below).

Show or hide

Everything shows until you choose. Tap the buttons under the search box to show only items with No gluten ingredients (as served, or with any milk except the ones named), items that Need a change (ask for a swap or a change), and items to Avoid (they contain gluten, or the restaurant says they aren't suitable because of how they're prepared). The Avoid list under the menu opens when you search or choose Avoid.

Favourites and notes

Open any item and tap ☆ Save. Favourites stay on this phone. If a new guide puts a favourite on the Avoid list, or drops it, you'll see a warning at the top until you remove it.

Every opened item and city place has a Your note box ("ask for a clean jug", "staff at King's Cross were careful"). Notes stay on this phone, and rows with one show ✎.

Share my favourites makes a link holding them all, notes included: keep it as a backup, or open it on another phone to add them there. Anything that has since gone, or is now on the Avoid list, isn't added.

Near me

The Near me chip under the search box lists which of the app's chains have a branch within a kilometre, nearest first, with its menu, directions and, where OpenStreetMap has them, its opening hours one tap away. The branches come from OpenStreetMap, which doesn't list every one; each chain's allergen guide covers its UK branches. The app keeps a list of them, refreshed each week, and works out what's near you on this phone, so it works without signal and your position goes nowhere.

Keep checking: with this on, each time the app opens, and at most every ten minutes while you move about, the app reads your position and the nearest chain shows on that chip ("Pret A Manger 300 m · open until 19:00") with no tap. Only the last answer is kept, on this phone, and it never asks for your location on its own: switch it on here, or when Near me first answers. On an iPhone the permission can lapse after a day unless Location Services for Safari is set to Allow; the chip then reads Near me again until you tap it. On a city's page, the chip names the nearest listed place still open today, from the list on this phone.

Map

The globe by the title opens a map with your position, which moves as you do, the branches of the app's chains around you, and every place on the city lists, UK and International, coloured as on their lists. Tap a pin for the distance, whether it's open today and directions, then Menu for a chain's menu or Open for a place's own words in its list. Drag the map anywhere in the UK and zoom in to a town to see the branches there; the round button brings it back to you.

Your position is followed only while the map is open and the app is in front, and stays on this phone: the branches come from the app's own list (from OpenStreetMap, which doesn't list every one, refreshed each week), so the pins show without signal. The map's background comes from OpenStreetMap while you have signal, so it can tell roughly which area you're looking at.

Tell me about new menus

A notification when a restaurant's guide changes, a badge on the app's icon, and a warning if a favourite now contains gluten. Also one when an update with new features arrives, which opens What's new. There's no server behind this app: your phone's notification address is saved by the app's owner, by hand, in the project's settings on GitHub, and nothing else leaves the phone.

Send this to whoever looks after the app. If that's you, add it as a line of the PUSH_SUBSCRIPTIONS secret under the project's Settings, Secrets and variables, Actions.

Show staff

Open any item and tap Show staff for a large-print card with the item, how to order it, and that you have coeliac disease. The screen stays lit while a card is open.

Build an order

On a restaurant's page, tap the Build an order chip under the search box (or Add to order in any opened item) to tick several things, such as a burger's patty and toppings, or a dish and its sauce, and show staff one card with all of them, how to order each, and the restaurant's own cross-contact warning. Only items with no gluten ingredients can be ticked: anything on the Avoid list is shown but can't be added, and if a new guide moves something in your order to the Avoid list, it's taken out and the builder says so. Orders stay on this phone.

Share an item, or report a mistake

Open any item and tap Share this item for a link that opens straight to it. Tap Something wrong? (on any item or city place) for a ready message with the restaurant, item and guide page, to send to whoever looks after the app. Guide page 7 in an opened item opens the restaurant's own guide, at that page where the phone's PDF viewer can, to show staff or to double-check.

UK franchises, UK cities and International

The switch at the top has three sections. UK franchises has the chains' menus, from their own allergen guides: the title under the switch chooses a restaurant, or All restaurants. UK cities (London, York, Sheffield, Poole and Worksop) and International (Berlin and Amsterdam) list places that are all gluten free, by their own word or a listing, or that say part of what they serve is, or that they make gluten free food in a separate kitchen: the title chooses the city, and each section goes back to the city you last looked at. The app also offers an International city when your phone is on its time.

A UK city's places were found through coeliac guides and Coeliac Sanctuary's listings; an International city's come from the coeliac society's list there. A UK city also lists the app's chains with branches in and around it, each with its menu one tap away. On a city's page, each tag says who makes that claim. Each row says whether it's open now (local time). Open a place for its own words, its addresses with map links, opening hours, and anything to ask about, such as oats. Open near me shows what's still open today, nearest first; Still open today, Near me and ★ Saved narrow the list on their own. Map shows the places as pins in the row's colours (OpenStreetMap, while you have signal); tap a pin, then Open. The German card or Dutch card chip opens the coeliac card, and About the tags and Before you go open under the introduction. Tap UK franchises to go back to the menus.

Travelling

Menus in other languages show each item in English with the original name underneath, so you can point to it when ordering. A coeliac card you can show staff is available in: . Open it from a city's page, or from a restaurant whose menu is in that language. Say it under each phrase reads it aloud in the phone's own voice for that language, which works without signal once the voice is installed.

I'm the app's owner

Uploading straight to the app needs a one-time GitHub key for this app's project. It's saved on this phone only.

  1. In Safari, open github.com/settings/personal-access-tokens/new.
  2. Name it Treat Temple upload and pick an expiry.
  3. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick gf-drinks.
  4. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  5. Tap Generate token, copy it and paste it below.
